About

Built Because It Needed to Exist.

Angrove gives serious questions a place that is private, lasting, and open to everyone. It's a personal project, built in the open, by one person who believes this particular use of AI should exist.

From the Founder

I'm Ryan, a web designer and now a software developer, with a lasting interest in philosophy, theology, and history. Angrove began when I learned that a capable language model could run entirely on a phone. That technical fact raised a more human question: what might someone finally be willing to ask if they knew the conversation could not leave their device?

I kept thinking about the questions people carry quietly, about faith, doubt, meaning, and truth, and about how little technology is built to help us stay with them. I wanted something that would make inquiry deeper rather than easier to avoid: a place to test an argument, follow a contradiction, and keep the ideas worth returning to.

I didn't build Angrove as a demonstration of what AI can do. I'm building it because I believe this particular use of it should exist, and because the people who need it most are often the least served by the technology we already have.

Principles

What Angrove Stands On

I

Sovereignty

You are the sole owner of your data. No cloud, no sync, no surveillance. What you think within Angrove stays under your control.

II

Permanence

Ideas are given structure and kept, like a library, so inquiry can deepen over time instead of disappearing into a feed.

III

Rigorous Inquiry

Inspired by the Scholastic method: test arguments at their strongest, examine contradictions, and keep conclusions open to revision.

IV

Accessibility

Angrove is free. The biggest questions there are shouldn't be paywalled. It's a resource built from conviction, not a product chasing revenue.

Built for the Questions You Keep to Yourself

Some questions are too personal to ask casually and too important to leave alone. Angrove was made with a few people in mind.

I

The Curious and the Doubting

For questions you feel you can't ask out loud, and the room to explore them honestly.

II

Students

For working through faith, identity, and meaning, with somewhere safe to bring the hard questions.

III

Missionaries and Remote Workers

A theological resource and study companion that works far from libraries, and far from signal.

IV

Anyone Who Has Kept a Question

If you've ever held on to a profound question because you didn't know where to take it, this is for you.
