Exhibit I · Study harness

Angrove

Public betaon iPhone

A private AI study harness for philosophy, history, and theology. Follow source-backed conversations, open contextual definitions, and build a persistent map of saved ideas on iPhone and Mac.

Visit Angrove

The project

I design and build Angrove around a continuing line of study: ask a question, follow its sources, explore a definition, and save an Insight to revisit on a map of related ideas. On-device AI and source retrieval keep inquiry private, with saved personal data encrypted on the device. On Mac, Boards bring conversations, notes, saved Insights, and images into one workspace.

Keep the way back within reach

Boards, conversations, the library, and study topics share one sidebar. Moving between kinds of work should never feel like leaving the room.

Test the default before choosing it

Before choosing Angrove’s reasoning default, I ran an 80-case evaluation. Both settings passed 74 of 80 cases, while reasoning roughly doubled latency, so I kept it off by default. This was a development configuration test, not a general accuracy claim.

Exhibit II · Research software

inVectus

In development

A local-first research tool for Mac. Map a collection of papers, find gaps in its coverage, and inspect the evidence behind each one.

The project

I’m building inVectus to help researchers explore a document collection and decide what to investigate next. Import a collection, search by meaning, or browse potential gaps on a map. Each candidate opens beside its supporting papers and score breakdown.

Platform
Mac
Built with
React, TypeScript, Rust
My role
Interface design and build, and its connection to a shared Rust engine
Status
Working prototype, MIT licensed

Source & project notes

Show the numbers behind a judgment.

a score you can argue with
Demand, coverage, and activity stay visible beside a flagged gap. The score is something to inspect, with the supporting papers close at hand.

A typical workflow

  4. saving ≠ deciding

Design notes

One local workspace

I design and build the desktop interface and its connection to a shared Rust engine. Search and indexing run locally, with resumable jobs and cancellation.

Keep a decision, not just a bookmark

Saving a gap and reviewing it are different actions. A persistent decision log keeps the evidence, notes, and review decisions available after rescanning the collection.

A claim should name its scope

“A gap in this collection” and “a novel idea” mean different things. The flag describes coverage within this collection; it does not establish that an idea is globally novel.

this collection, not the world
