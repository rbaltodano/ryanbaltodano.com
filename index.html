inVectus, research in context

A local-first research desktop app in development, validated locally on macOS. I design and build its React/TypeScript interface and shared Rust engine.

Explore possible gaps on an interactive map, inspect supporting evidence, and save review decisions. Bookmarks and decisions stay separate, with saved records retained across rescans. Indexing is resumable and cancellable; assessments make their collection scope and uncertainty explicit.
