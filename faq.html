The App

About Angrove

What is Angrove?

A private study companion for iPhone. You ask serious questions about philosophy, theology, and history, and Angrove answers with the primary sources in its Library. Terms you save become Insights, and your Insights grow into a map of your thinking called the Insight Tree.

The User Guide walks through every part of it.

When can I get it?

Not yet. Angrove is in active development and isn’t publicly released, and email signup isn’t open. An open TestFlight beta comes before the App Store release. Launch Status shows where things stand.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Angrove is free, with no subscription and no paywalled features, and that’s meant to stay true.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s nothing to sign up for or log in to.

Which iPhones will it run on?

It’s built and tested on iPhone 17, with 8 GB of memory. Final device requirements will be set once the launch model is chosen, and shared before launch.

Who makes it?

Ryan, one person building it in the open. You can read why on the About page.

Privacy

Your Data

Does anything I type leave my phone?

No. The model that answers you runs on your iPhone, and your conversations, Insights, and Insight Tree are stored only on it. The app makes no network requests to answer a question or build your tree. Its only network code downloads the model itself.

The source is public, so you can check it yourself.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the model is on your phone, Angrove answers in airplane mode.

Are my conversations backed up?

Angrove keeps its data in the app’s own storage, like any other app. If you use iCloud Backup, iOS may include that data in your device backup. You can also export your conversations to a file in Settings → Privacy & Data. An export holds conversations only, not Insights or settings.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on App Lock in Settings → Privacy & Data to require Face ID, and choose how soon it locks.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a conversation removes it from the app. Clear Insight Tree removes saved Insights and tree data but keeps your conversations. Deleting the app removes everything Angrove stored on your phone.

Answers

The Model and Its Sources

What model does Angrove use?

Gemma 4 E4B, instruction-tuned, from the LiteRT Community package. It runs on your iPhone’s GPU through Google’s LiteRT-LM runtime and is about 3.7 GB. It isn’t fine-tuned in the current build, and the launch model is still being tested. More detail.

Where do answers come from?

The Library holds 37 primary works and 51,836 passages, from Scripture and the Church Fathers to Aristotle, the councils, the confessions, and classical history. Answers cite the passages they draw on, and every source opens in full so you can read it in its own words.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. Answers can make mistakes, so check important claims against their sources. That’s why the sources are one tap away.

Is it a replacement for a pastor or counselor?

No. Angrove is a tool for private study and reflection. It is not a substitute for professional counseling, spiritual direction, or clergy.

What happens in a very long conversation?

The model holds about 4,000 tokens of a conversation at a time. Type /compact to summarize older parts so Angrove can keep going. You still see everything on screen.

Why does it do one thing at a time?

Because it runs entirely on your phone. Anything else you ask for waits in line, and Model Tasks shows that line so you can stop, remove, or reorder what’s next. See Model Tasks in the guide.
