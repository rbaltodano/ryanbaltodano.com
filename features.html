02 · Branch

Follow a Line of Thought Without Losing Your Place

A conversation can hold branches side by side. Start one from a quoted concept or a copy of an answer, and pursue the objection, the tangent, or the harder version of the question. The original line stays exactly where you left it.

Aquinas on Justice

What does Aquinas say about the virtue of justice?

Justice is about our relationship with others: giving each what they are due. Aquinas calls it the chief of the moral virtues, because it orders us toward the common good.

“giving each their due”

Is mercy ever unjust, then?

Not for Aquinas. Mercy doesn't take away what is owed; it gives more than is owed. So mercy goes beyond justice without working against it.

03 · Keep

Keep the Terms That Matter

Tap an underlined term for a definition written for the context of your conversation, not a dictionary entry. Quote it, branch from it, or save it as an Insight.

Saved Insights gather in your Insight Library and join the conversation's Insight Tree. Save the same term from a different conversation and its new meaning is added to the same card.

04 · Map

The Insight Tree: A Map of What You've Pursued

Not a map of everything, only of the questions you asked and the ideas you chose to keep. It grows as you study, it's built entirely on your iPhone, and over time it shows how your thinking changed.

Node Concepts

Subjects Take Shape on Their Own

After each answer, Angrove quietly reviews the turn in the background and may add one Node Concept: the broader subject the conversation has reached. Whether it becomes a new node or joins an existing one is decided by how closely its meaning matches the subjects already in your tree.

It never saves Insights for you. Those are always your choice.

Insights

Insights Are the Terms You Keep

An Insight is a definition you saved from a conversation. It settles near the Node Concepts it relates to, in that conversation's tree and in your global tree, which gathers every Insight you've saved.

Changes to the global tree wait for you: when new bookmarks are ready to be placed, you choose when to update it.

Midpoint

Find the Idea Between Ideas

Select two to eight Insights or Node Concepts and drag a handle toward the ones that should weigh most. Angrove proposes five candidate concepts, and your phone picks the one whose meaning sits closest to the weighted center of your selection.

It's a calculation in the space of meanings, not a request to blend some words together.

Study

Study a Subject in Three Dimensions

Choose Study on any Node Concept and the camera swings down to eye level. Its Insights spread out around it in 3D, and the rest of the tree fades away.

Drag the ring to turn it like a turntable, tap an Insight to bring it forward, and exit to land back exactly where you were in the tree.

Model Tasks

One Task at a Time, in the Open

Everything the model does runs through one queue you can see. Your questions and definitions come first. Updating the Insight Tree waits until you've paused, and steps aside when you ask something new.

Tap the status in the dock to see what's running, reorder what's next, or cancel it.

Getting the most from it

A Few Habits That Help

Ask It As You Hold It

Bring the real question, doubts and all. Angrove represents your view at its strongest before it questions it.

Push Back

If an answer doesn't hold, say why. It is built to revise its conclusions when your reasoning warrants it.

Branch, Don't Overwrite

Take the objection or tangent into a branch, so the main line stays clear to come back to.

Save Only What You'll Return To

Insights shape your tree. Keep the terms that matter and let the rest go.

It can still be wrong. Angrove can make mistakes, including about sources. Treat it as a serious study companion, not an authority beyond question. How the model works
